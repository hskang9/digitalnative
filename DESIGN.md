## Composition and behavior

The opening view immediately establishes the complete planted tower. The desktop composition places architecture left and editorial copy right; mobile puts the tower above readable foreground copy. No entrance click, initial camera flight, or scroll is required to see the garden. Four main storeys and two garden wings contain the product gallery, workshop, partnership pavilion, material library, Press room, and conversation court. Real HTML content is mounted on CSS3D wall surfaces. The walls retain native text selection, links, video controls, and scrolling. A transparent header and persistent space navigation reach every section directly.

Choosing a room triggers a direct 650–950ms camera transition. A separate, saved **Scroll tour** toggle is off by default. When enabled, one native document scrollbar drives the complete route: three garden views, Products, Practice, Partnership, Stack, Press, and Contact including the footer. The stage stays sticky. Each room gets a stationary reading segment sized from its actual content overflow and projected scale, plus short arrival and departure pauses. There is no wheel cancellation, snapping, or nested-scroll trap in tour mode. Navigation seeks to the chosen room within the same route; scrolling backward reverses it. Resizing and switching languages preserve the current segment and relative reading position. Turning the tour off retains the current destination and restores independent native room scrolling. Hash URLs support direct links and browser Back/Forward. Turning off camera motion also turns off the scroll tour; enabling the tour re-enables motion. The operating system's reduced-motion preference disables both animations. Daylight can switch to golden hour.

A local tower illustration remains until the first successful WebGL frame. If WebGL is unavailable, all content appears in normal document flow. A small garden pool has subtle ripples; expensive real-time reflection and sky-environment passes were removed for the illustrated direction. Plants are instanced, resolution is capped, rendering pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs, inactive CSS3D walls are hidden/inert, and GPU resources are released on unmount. The SVG favicon matches the teal/ochre architectural monogram.
